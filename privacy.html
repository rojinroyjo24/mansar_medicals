Privacy Policy

How MANSAR Medical protects your data and maintains strict GxP confidentiality standards.

1. Information We Collect

MANSAR Medical collects personal and organizational data submitted voluntarily through inquiry forms, consultation requests, and contract executions. This includes contact details, company information, and technical regulatory documents.

2. Data Confidentiality & GxP Protection

All clinical dossiers, trade secrets, formulation data, and regulatory submittals provided by clients are treated as strictly confidential under non-disclosure agreements (NDAs) and applicable laws of the Republic of Kazakhstan and international GxP standards.

3. Use of Personal Data

We process collected data solely to deliver regulatory consulting services, communicate project status, prepare registration packages for competent state authorities, and manage client inquiries.

4. Data Retention & Security

MANSAR Medical maintains physical, technical, and administrative safeguards to protect client records from unauthorized access, loss, or disclosure. Records are retained in accordance with statutory requirements for pharmaceutical and medical device archives.

5. Your Legal Rights

You have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal contact records maintained by MANSAR Medical by contacting us at info@mansar-med.com.
